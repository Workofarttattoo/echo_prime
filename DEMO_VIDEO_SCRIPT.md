## 🎤 VOICEOVER TIPS

### **Recording Your Voice:**
1. Use a quiet room (closet works great for sound dampening)
2. Use a decent mic (even iPhone earbuds are okay)
3. Speak clearly and slowly (easier than you think!)
4. Record in segments (one scene at a time)
5. Leave 2 seconds of silence before/after each segment

### **Script Delivery Tips:**
- Emphasize key numbers: "**82 percent** accuracy"
- Pause for effect: "Every decision is traceable. [pause] Every step is verifiable."
- Vary your tone: Excited for achievements, serious for problems
- Smile while talking (it comes through in your voice!)

### **Using AI Voice (ElevenLabs):**
1. Choose "Professional Male" or "Professional Female" voice
2. Adjust stability: 75%
3. Adjust clarity: 85%
4. Generate in segments, then combine

## 📊 TEXT-TO-VIDEO GENERATION TIPS

### **For Best Results:**

**Prompt Structure:**
```
[Main Subject] + [Action/State] + [Environment] + [Style] + [Technical specs]
```

**Example:**
"Futuristic holographic AI brain [subject] with transparent neural pathways lighting up [action] in a dark tech lab [environment], cinematic 4K professional [style]"

### **Quality Settings:**
- **Resolution**: 1920×1080 minimum (4K if possible)
- **Duration**: 4-6 seconds per generation (loop or extend as needed)
- **Frame Rate**: 24 or 30 fps
- **Style**: Consistent across all clips (same art direction)

### **Time-Saving Tips:**
1. Generate all clips at once (batch processing)
2. Use same style prompt across clips for consistency
3. Generate longer than needed, trim in editing
4. Re-use good clips (e.g., background animations)
